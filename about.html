ROOTED IN THE MAASAI MARA

Welcome to our world.

Mara Green Heart is a place to experience the Maasai Mara in a more personal, unhurried way.

Here, the beauty of the landscape sets the rhythm. You can rest under canvas, linger with a hot drink, head out for a day of exploration, and return to the warmth of camp. It is the kind of place where big horizons and everyday moments belong together.

Whether you have come for adventure, the landscape, or simply to reconnect with yourself and your companions, there is something special about finding a place that feels grounded in its surroundings.

LIFE AT CAMP

Where evenings linger.

There's a particular kind of calm that arrives after a day outside. The sky begins to soften, the fire draws people together, and there is time to share the best parts of the day.

Our images capture simple, welcoming camp spaces — places to sit, eat, talk, and enjoy the quiet. What makes it memorable is never just the setting. It's the feeling of being there.

PEOPLE & PLACE

A living culture. A shared story.

The Maasai Mara is known around the world for its wildlife, but the region is also home to living communities with a rich cultural heritage.

We encourage guests to approach cultural experiences with openness and respect: to listen, learn, ask questions, and appreciate the people who call this landscape home. Ask our team about culturally respectful local experiences that may be arranged during your stay.
