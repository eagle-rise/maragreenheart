EXPERIENCE THE MARA YOUR WAY

Big adventures. Small, beautiful moments.

These are experiences you can enquire about when planning your visit. Some may involve external operators, guides, transport, or separate fees, so arrangements are always confirmed individually.

THE WILD SIDE

Safari game drives

There is nothing quite like setting out into the vast landscapes of the Maasai Mara. Every game drive offers a chance to experience the bush from a new perspective, with the possibility of wonderful wildlife sightings along the way. Ask our team what drive arrangements are available for your stay.

Vehicle, guide, park fees and schedules must be confirmed.Ask about this experience

AN EARLY START WORTH MAKING

Hot-air balloon experiences

For those dreaming of the Mara from above, a balloon excursion is an unforgettable way to see the savannah in the morning light. Balloon flights are subject to third-party operator availability, weather, and additional charges.

Advance planning and operator confirmation are recommended.Ask about this experience

PEOPLE, TRADITIONS, STORIES

Maasai cultural encounters

Explore the human story of this remarkable region through respectful cultural exchange. Learn about Maasai traditions, dress, and ways of life in a way that values local voices and genuine connection. Contact the camp to enquire about appropriate community-based activities.

Details of visits and any contributions are confirmed in advance.Ask about this experience

SLOW DOWN & LOOK CLOSER

Nature walks & viewpoints

A walk changes how you notice the Mara. Look closer at plants, textures, smaller details, and the landscapes around camp. Guided outdoor walks should be organised only in appropriate, safe areas with qualified local guidance.

Routes, guides, safety arrangements, and availability are by enquiry.Ask about this experience

FOOD IS PART OF THE STORY

Local flavours & camp dining

Pull up a chair. Share a meal. Let the day unfold again through conversation. The photos capture colourful food and a communal covered dining space, inviting guests to slow down and savour the moment.

Meal options, dietary needs, and inclusions are confirmed with the camp.Ask about this experience

THE MAGIC OF THE EVENING

Fireside gatherings

There is a lovely simplicity in gathering near the fire, wrapped in the cool evening air. From stories to quiet reflection, some of the best moments happen when there is nowhere else you need to be.

Fire use is dependent on camp operations and local conditions.Ask about this experience
